Boom Belly Baby

The Legend of Boom Belly (Fanfiction Story of Puppet Master)
Backstory: A 1940s tragedy of obsessed love. After discovering the Puppet Master’s heart belonged to another, she sought a medical exit. A frantic "rescue" by the Master caused a fatal fall onto a landmine. To save her spirit, the Master transplanted her soul into this wooden masterpiece—forever carrying the blast that ended her life.

Powers & Mechanics:
Belly Hatch: A mechanical surgical scar that unfolds into a brass rolling track.
Fire Opals: Spherical red explosives that clack forward from her hollow core.
The Wish-Snap: A snap of her wooden fingers triggers the "Wishmaster" detonation.

Aesthetic: Articulated wood-grain joints paired with a vintage leather jacket.

Co-Creation Audit:
Fonda: Concept, Backstory, Lyric Hook, Design (85%)
Gemini AI: Narrative Polishing, Image/Prompt Support (15%)
