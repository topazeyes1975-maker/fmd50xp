  • Origin: Afghanistan (A synthetic amalgamation of classic regional folklore and mythology).
  • Classification: Mythological Hybrid / Chimeric Guardian.
  • Core Philosophy: A physical manifestation of contrasting regional tales—blending ancient Zoroastrian terrors, trickster Jinns, musical predators, and benevolent celestial protectors into a single unified form.
      • Anatomical Breakdown (The Merge)
    • Core Framework: The massive, stone-textured, and hyper-muscular body of a Div (Ancient Giant Demon).
    • Stance & Foundation: The spherical, hoof-like "round feet" of the Pai Girdak (Hammam Jinn).
    • Olfactory & Vocal Apparatus: The long, multi-perforated flute-snout of the Siwānis (Musical Beast of Kabul).
    • Facial Features: The weathered, striking countenance and fiery-red mane of the Madar-i-Al (Mother Al).
    • Appendages: The radiant, peacock-feathered wings and loyal canine ears of the majestic Simurgh (The Wise Guardian Bird).
      Attributes & Abilities
    • Continuity Matrix: Much like a perfect circle where all points are equal, this entity functions as a balanced ecosystem, utilizing every distinct anatomical feature in tandem.
    • Resonance Haunting: Uses its Siwānis nose to blow air through its multi-channeled snout, generating an enchanting, hypnotic melody that lures targets into a trance before they even see its monstrous form.
    • Grounded Paradox: The heavy, low-center-of-gravity Div body combined with the circular Pai Girdak feet allows it to remain absolutely unmovable on any terrain, though its movements defy standard bipedal physics.
    • Celestial Lift & Aegis: Despite its heavy, subterranean stone chassis, the glowing Simurgh wings grant it effortless, magical flight and high-altitude mobility.
    • Primal Acute Awareness: The Simurgh dog ears offer hyper-sensitive acoustic tracking, ensuring it can never be ambushed, while the intimidating gaze of the Madar-i-Al face evokes natural psychological dread.
