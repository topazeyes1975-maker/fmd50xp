    VITAL STATS
  • ​Style Name: The Celestial Waterfall Ponytail
  • ​Element: Air (Though symbolized by the Water Bearer, Aquarius is an Air sign)
  • ​Ruler Planet: Uranus (Modern) / Saturn (Traditional)
    ​📊 CORE ATTRIBUTES ​
  • Flow of style: Fluid and cascading. It mimics a rushing waterfall, starting from a high, structural base and spilling backward into smooth, wavy currents.
  • ​Strength of hold: 88/100
  • ​Structure of creation: 45/100
  • ​Likely it can be done without supports: Unlikely. Because of the massive, gravity-defying height of the high ponytail base and the dense wrap, it would almost certainly require an internal hair donut, mesh padding, or wire structural supports to keep it from sagging.
    ​⚡ SPECIAL TRAITS
  • ​Chemical needed: Heavy-duty freezing spray (for the sleek side panels), high-shine glossing serum (to give it that metallic, liquid-like sheen), and temporary or permanent aqua/metallic blue hair dye.

  • ​Length of hair need: Very Long (Ex-long/Waist length). A massive amount of length is required to wrap the thick base multiple times and leave enough hair to cascade down the back.

  • Perception / Tracking: 👁️ 8.5/10 Massive, light-sensitive sugar glider eyes grant superb night vision, paired with acute wolf-like hearing to pinpoint movement.
  • Defense / Durability: 🛡️ 6.5/10 While her upper torso is lean and built for flight, her heavy, segmented scorpion tail and lower wolf-like legs provide robust defense and stability.

  • 🌓 Jigsaw Shroud (Sunset Jigsaw Trait) When stationary among autumn leaves, sun-dappled shadows, or rocky terracotta terrain, her skin pattern visually breaks up her silhouette. Enemies suffer a massive penalty to detecting her unless she moves.

  • 🧪 Neurotoxic Stinger (Scorpion Trait) Her primary weapon is her heavy, armored tail. A single strike delivers a fast-acting neurotoxin that paralyzes muscles. She can control the dosage, using it either to incapacitate an opponent or as a lethal last resort.

  • Preferred Range: Medium to Close Ambush.
  • Tactics: She stalks from the high branches during twilight, uses her incredible vision to select a target, glides down completely silently, strikes quickly with her stinger, and retreats back into the canopy until the venom takes effect.

Faremanshaw is this wolf name

    📜 Master Outline: The Twin Hounds of Fairmenshaw A Gothic Dark Fantasy Thriller
    🪵 The Soul of the Creature
  • The Visual: A massive, ancient spirit creature made of living wood, bark, and dark, layered leaves. His gnarled legs look like powerful tree roots, making him a literal extension of the forest.
  • The Ability: He operates with a single mind that can seamlessly split into two identical, dual-striking wolves mid-attack.
  • The Spiritual Law: He acts as a supernatural guardian who is bound to answer the cries of the innocent whenever his ghost sisters chant from the misty deep.
    🎬 The Story Blueprint
  • Act I: The Mother's Gift & The Theft The story begins in the past. The mother carves a beautiful wooden dog for her children. It is infused with her love. Driven by toxic, obsessive jealousy because the kids are "stealing" her time, the villain steals the wooden dog. He uses it as bait to lure the sister to the well, destroying the family and forcing the young boy to flee.
  • Act II: The Detective's Amnesia Decades later, the boy is a grit-and-glory detective in his 40s with no memory of his early childhood. While clearing out his attic, he discovers the dust-covered wooden dog. Touching it shatters his amnesia just enough to trigger vivid nightmares of a lost sister. Believing she is still alive out there, he launches an official missing-persons investigation.
  • Act III: The Trap & The Well Guided by a prophetic dream, the detective tracks the clues to an ancient, mossy stone well deep in the Fairmenshaw woods. He finds the old, dried-up bones of his past off to the side and peers into the mist, catching a glimpse of a little girl's spirit. Before he can react, the ancient villain springs his trap. Having tracked the detective, the villain brings a local police force and an angry mob of superstitious villagers. The villain screams: "He's back to his old hunting grounds to steal your children!" The panicked mob surges forward, overpowers the detective, and hurls him down into the well.
  • Act IV: Rebirth & Justice As he plummets into the blackness, the shouting cuts to silence. The ghost sisters begin to sing their haunting lullaby. The magic activates, fusing his human body with the ancient roots and leaves. He returns to the surface as the wooden wolf to protect the woods and track his framer.
  • The Climax: The Final Mic-Drop At the very end of the story, he corners the unraveled, screaming villain by the well. Standing tall as the calm, emotionless judge, the detective delivers your killer final line: "I heard in documentaries that serial killers don't have any emotion. So it's a good thing you are so emotional... because I would probably be scared of my competition." The wolf splits, and justice is served.
