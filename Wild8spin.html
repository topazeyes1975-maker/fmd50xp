How to Play: Mystic Pendulum Eights

Welcome to Mystic Pendulum Eights, a strategic card game where classic matching mechanics collide with an unpredictable alignment wheel of fate. You are playing against a computer intelligence to see who can completely clear their hand first.

    The Core Rules
  • The Match Criteria: You must play a card from your hand that matches either the Suit or the Value of the card currently face-up on the discard pile.
  • Drawing Cards: If you do not have a matching card in your hand, you must tap the deck to draw a card.
  • The Turn Vector: Unlike traditional Crazy Eights, drawing a card immediately concludes your current turn. Even if the card you just drew is a perfect match, you must hold it in your hand and wait for the track to return to you on your next turn before you can play it.
    The Crazy Eight & The Pendulum
  • The 8s are completely wild, but playing one is a massive gamble. When you or the computer plays an Eight, the game state shifts into an Alignment Spin:
  • Play the Eight: Lay down an 8 of any suit.
  • Execute the Rotation: Tap the center SPIN button to release the pendulum needle.
  • Accept the Verdict: The pendulum will land on one of the cosmic outcomes that instantly alters the hands or the board:
  • If it lands on YES: The Eight is accepted! You safely mutate the current suit vector to any suit you choose.
  • If it lands on NO: The Eight is rejected! The card is pulled back into your hand, and the match criteria resets to what it was before.
  • If it lands on MINUS 2 or MINUS 3: Absolute fortune! The engine instantly purges 2 or 3 cards out of your hand and buries them back in the deck. (Yes, you can dynamically win the game right here if this clears your hand!)
  • If it lands on ADD 2 or ADD 3: A penalty payload! Your opponent is forced to draw 2 or 3 extra penalty cards into their hand.
  • If it lands on SPIN AGAIN: The alignment is locked in a loop. You must spin the pendulum needle again until a decisive verdict is met.
    Securing Victory

    The first intelligence to successfully drop their hand down to 0 cards completely purges the matrix and secures total victory. Watch out for the computer saving an Eight for its last card—the pendulum might just force them to take it back!
