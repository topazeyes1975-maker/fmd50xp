HOLE & HOLD

1. Scoop Captives: Drive your hole around the field to scoop targets into your inventory.

Small (Flees)

2. Deposit & Contain: Drive to your Home Base pen (top-left) while the gate is open to unload your haul.

3. Lock & Digest: Lock the gate chain tether! Drive inside the locked pen to swallow captives for coins and size growth.

4. Beware Breakouts: Strong captives trapped inside will attack the chain gate until you enter to eat them.

5. Controls: Drag with your Finger / Mouse (or WASD/Arrows). Press Spacebar or tap GATE button to lock/unlock.
