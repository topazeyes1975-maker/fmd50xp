How the Pet-Link Bracelet Works

The Pet-Link is a collaborative interface between user and companion. Upon receiving a message, the creature performs a unique "attention-grabbing" sequence: it taps its paw to gently pat the wrist, followed by a deliberate nod of its head, signaling me to look down and read the incoming information.

Once the message has been read, a simple delete command initiates the creature’s "feeding" routine, turning the act of clearing a notification into a rewarding, playful interaction.
