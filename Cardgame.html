Global Persistent Board

How to Play & Global Rules

Welcome to a shared world-board version of the classic bluffing game. The primary goal of the game is to discard cards without being caught lying!

1. The Core Rules:

  • The game cycles through ranks in order: Aces, 2s, 3s... up to Kings, then loops back to Aces.
  • On your turn, you must declare how many cards of the current target rank you are face-down discarding.
  • If you don't have that card, you must Bluff and play completely different cards while lying about it!
  • If someone suspects a lie, they shout "STRONZATE!"

2. The Live Drop-In System:

Unlike standard games that reset every time you close the window, this board is hooked up to a live global database:

  • Make a Move and Leave: You can load this page up, play a hand honestly or launch a massive bluff, and leave!
  • Asynchronous Visitors: Random visitors from entirely different computers can drop onto the board later and see your exact move.
  • Persistent State: They can choose to continue the cycle or challenge the previous action left by a total stranger.
