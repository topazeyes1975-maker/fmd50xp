How to Play & Global Rules

Welcome to a shared world-board version of the classic bluffing game. The primary goal of the game is to discard your cards by matching the target rank—or successfully faking it!

1. The Core Rules:

  • The game cycles through ranks in order: Aces, 2s, 3s... up to Kings, then loops back to Aces.
  • On your turn, you must declare how many cards of the current target rank you are face-down discarding.
  • If you don't have that card, you must Bluff and play completely different cards while pretending they are correct.
  • If someone suspects a lie, they shout "STRONZATE!" (the Italian equivalent of calling out the bluff). If caught, the liar takes the pile. If the play was honest, the challenger takes the penalty pile.

2. The Live Drop-In System:

Unlike standard games that reset every time you close the window, this board is hooked up to a live cloud database brain. It acts as a persistent community table:

  • Make a Move and Leave: You can load this page up, play a hand honestly or launch a massive bluff, and then completely close your browser or walk away.
  • Asynchronous Visitors: Random visitors from entirely different computers can drop onto this exact link hours later. They will see the exact pile size and target rank you left behind.
  • Persistent State: They can choose to continue the cycle or challenge the previous action. The database updates in real-time, refreshing automatically every 5 seconds, and will never restart unless an admin hits the manual override button.
