Welcome to Cipher Codec

This tool allows you to design your own custom visual alphabet and 40 high-frequency cryptogram word symbols to translate text or power your 3D bluffing Scrabble board.

How to Use:

  • Create Your Symbols: Tap on any letter or word box to draw. Word boxes support Side A (Front) and Side B (Back) designs!
  • Save to the Cloud: Type a custom passcode or name into the box at the bottom, then click Save to lock your complete code online.
  • Load Your Saved Code: Type your passcode into the box and click Load to retrieve your drawings anytime.
  • Launch Game: Once your code is saved, hit Play 3D Scrabble Game to launch the board!

Important Note on Security & Privacy:

This platform operates on a strict privacy-first model. Your cipher keys are saved securely in the cloud using only the custom passcode you create—whether that is a name, a phrase, or a sequence of numbers. We do not collect usernames, email addresses, or personal data, which means your passcode cannot be recovered or reset if it is lost. Please write down or remember your passcode carefully; if you lose it, your saved drawings cannot be retrieved.
